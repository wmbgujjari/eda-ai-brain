CONSUMER SERVICE POLICY

Consumer Category

Domestic consumers are residential electricity consumers. Commercial consumers
are consumers using electricity for business or commercial activities. Industrial
consumers are consumers using electricity for manufacturing and industrial
operations.

New Connection

A domestic consumer applying for a new electricity connection must submit the
required application form and valid identity and address proof. The application
is reviewed by the electricity distribution company before the connection is
approved.

The standard processing time for a new domestic electricity connection is
7 working days after all required documents have been submitted.

Commercial consumers may require additional documentation depending on the
connected load and nature of the business. The processing time can vary based
on field verification and required approvals.

Meter Reading

Consumer meter readings are recorded periodically by the electricity
distribution company. The recorded meter reading is used to calculate the
consumer's electricity consumption for the billing period.

Monthly Consumption

Monthly electricity consumption is calculated based on the difference between
the current meter reading and the previous meter reading.

For example, if the previous meter reading is 1250 units and the current meter
reading is 1500 units, the monthly consumption is 250 units.

Billing

The electricity bill is generated based on the consumer's recorded consumption
and the applicable tariff.

Domestic and commercial consumers may have different tariff structures.
Additional charges, taxes, or applicable adjustments may be included in the
final bill.

Consumer Complaints

Consumers can raise complaints related to billing, meter readings, power
supply, new connections, or other electricity services.

A complaint should contain the consumer ID and sufficient information about
the issue so that the electricity distribution company can investigate and
resolve the complaint.
